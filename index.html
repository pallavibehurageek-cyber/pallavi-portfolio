01 ABOUT

Curiosity built into
working systems.

I’m a prefinal-year B.Tech student in Electronics and Communication Engineering (2024–2028), interested in the intersection of digital design, computer architecture and hardware acceleration.

My project work includes a continuous-flow 4096-point FFT/IFFT accelerator, a RISC-V SoC with a custom fixed-point accelerator, AXI4-Lite peripheral IP and a modular SystemVerilog verification environment.

I also explore quantization, mixed-precision arithmetic and CNN datapaths. RF/antenna and embedded systems remain part of my broader ECE interests.
