01 ABOUT

Curiosity translated
into working hardware.

A design-oriented approach to learning digital systems: understand the architecture, implement the logic, verify the behaviour and study the implementation trade-offs.

I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (2024–2028). My strongest current interests are VLSI/RTL design, functional verification, computer architecture and hardware acceleration.

My projects span a continuous-flow FFT/IFFT accelerator, a processor-based RISC-V SoC, memory-mapped AXI4-Lite peripheral IP and a modular SystemVerilog verification environment. I also explore hardware-aware quantization, mixed-precision arithmetic and CNN datapaths.

Outside my main digital-hardware focus, I have worked on embedded control, IoT, RF and antenna-related ECE projects.
